## Version check

- consumerfinance.gov lists the Regulation B versions as July 21, 2026 (marked "current regulation"), June 30, 2026, Aug. 2, 2024, Aug. 29, 2023, April 19, 2023, Jan. 1, 2022 and Jan. 1, 2018. Each CFPB page saved here shows the banner "This version is the current regulation."
- The eCFR versioner shows that the July 21, 2026 final rule amended Supplement I and sections 1002.4, 1002.6, 1002.8 and 1002.15. It did not amend 1002.2, 1002.9 or Appendix C.
- I diffed the eCFR point-in-time text for 2026-07-20 against 2026-10-29. The 1002.9 commentary and the 2(c) commentary are textually identical before and after the amendment. The amendment's changes to Supplement I fall in the commentary to 2(p), 4(b), 6(a) and 8(a), which are not used here.
- The eCFR and CFPB texts of the 9(b)(2) commentary match apart from whitespace.

## Not used as authority

- CFPB Circulars 2022-03 and 2023-03 were withdrawn on May 12, 2025. They are not cited.
- The current Form C-1 checklist does not contain the reason "We do not grant credit to any applicant on the terms and conditions you request." That line is not in the current Appendix C text, so no case relies on it. The closest current sample-form language that names a creditor threshold is Form C-2, "Your Income: is below our minimum requirement."
